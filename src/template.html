Our Philosophy

At Giordano's, we believe Italian cuisine is a living language. We take deeply rooted traditions and translate them through a contemporary lens. You will find the comforts of heritage cooking, presented with rigorous seasonal shifts and modern refinement.

The Chefs

Our kitchen is guided by an ongoing dialogue between our chefs. Elena Giordano brings intuitive, generational knowledge and Damán Baste brings technical precision honed in global fine-dining establishments.

The Experience

We invite you to experience our rotating tasting menu in a dining room designed for relaxed refinement. Pull up a chair, share a glass from our curated cellar, and taste the intersection of tradition and tomorrow.
